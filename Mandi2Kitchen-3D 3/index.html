QUICK ANSWERS

Good to know
before you order.

How are product rates decided?

Rates vary with the market. We confirm availability, current unit rates and any applicable charges in WhatsApp. Final billing follows the market purchase rate.

How do I place an order?

Add products and quantities in the catalogue, then send your delivery details to our WhatsApp Business account.

Will every product be available?

Availability changes with the market. We confirm each requested item before accepting the order.

When is my order confirmed?

We send the availability, rates and delivery details for review. The order moves forward after you approve the details in WhatsApp.

What is the minimum order?

The minimum is 10 kg combined across products ordered in kilograms. Items ordered by piece, bunch, box or crate do not count toward the 10 kg minimum.

How are delivery charges and timing handled?

Coverage, delivery timing and any applicable charges are confirmed for your location and requested date before you approve.

What if an item is unavailable?

We’ll confirm availability with you. Substitutions are not assumed; tell us your preference and approve any changes in WhatsApp.

How can I report a delivery issue?

If an item is missing or not as requested, message us with your PO or order reference, affected item, quantity and a photo if available. Contact order support on WhatsApp.

How do I check an order’s status?

Status updates are handled directly in WhatsApp, not through a live website tracker. Request an update with your order reference.
