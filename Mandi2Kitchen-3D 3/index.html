QUICK ANSWERS

Good to know
before you order.

How are product rates decided?

Rates vary with the market. Send your quantities first; we confirm the current rate and availability in WhatsApp before fulfillment.

How do I place an order?

Add products and quantities in the catalogue, then send your delivery details to our WhatsApp Business account.

Will every product be available?

Availability changes with the market. We confirm each requested item before accepting the order.

Can I request a product not listed?

Yes. Use the custom produce request on the catalogue page to send the product, quantity and specifications.
