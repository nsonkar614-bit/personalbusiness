MANDI2KITCHEN — SIMPLE WHATSAPP ORDERING WEBSITE

This is a static website. No database, login, API key, server or build step is required.

PAGES
- index.html — homepage and business information
- products.html — vegetable catalogue and order builder
- partner.html — restaurant partner inquiry
- how-it-works.html — order and delivery process
- service-areas.html — current service regions and neighborhoods

HOW ORDERS WORK
Customers choose products and quantities, enter their delivery details, and send the order to Mandi2Kitchen WhatsApp Business. Current rates and availability are confirmed in WhatsApp. The order draft stays in the customer's browser until they send it.

HOSTING
Upload index.html, products.html, partner.html, how-it-works.html, service-areas.html, app.js, partner.js, styles.css, visual-refresh.css and favicon.svg to the public folder of any static website host. Keep all files together. There is no backend setup or deployment command.

Before publishing, confirm the WhatsApp number, email address, service areas and delivery information.
